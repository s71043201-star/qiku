# 第二晚「點名」生圖清單

共 8 張。在 ChatGPT 的「Generate Scratchboard Image」對話裡，每張都先貼「共用風格」，再接該張的描述。
生好後存成下表的檔名（JPG 或 PNG 都可以），放進 `七窟_原型/img/`，再請 Claude 接進遊戲。

## 共用風格（每張都接在最前面）

```
Scratchboard illustration: fine white lines scratched out of a solid black background, dense cross-hatching, engraving-like texture. Only three colors: black, aged off-white, and a single accent of dull dark red used sparingly. High contrast, large areas of pure black. Stylized and unsettling, not photorealistic. Setting: a Taiwanese nursing home, 1990. Square image. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signs, no symbols.
```

### photo_staff

```
An old group photograph from 1953 lying on a wooden table, seen from above, slightly tilted. In the photo about twenty staff members in plain white uniforms stand in three rows in front of a two-storey building. One figure in the front row has been carefully cut out of the photo with scissors, leaving a person-shaped hole; only a sliver of one cheek with a small mole remains at the edge of the cut. At the far end of the back row stands a young man of about twenty-five holding a mop, the only one not in uniform. Faces are small and simplified. The photo has worn corners and a crease.
```

### office

```
A small director's office at night, seen from the doorway. A heavy wooden desk with a hard-cover register book lying open under the narrow beam of light from the corridor. The bottom desk drawer has a new padlock, picked out in dull dark red. A filing cabinet, a wall clock with no numerals, a window with rain on the glass. Nobody in the room. Almost everything is black; only the desk top and the padlock are lit.
```

### bed_empty

```
A single iron-frame bed in a small nursing home room in grey morning light. The bed has been freshly made, the sheet pulled perfectly flat and tight, the pillow squared, no personal belongings left anywhere. The door stands open. On the bedside table, one small folded dull dark red handkerchief is the only object. Empty, quiet, too tidy.
```

From the gap under the door a very small child's hand reaches out, and the young man's little finger is hooked around the child's little finger in a promise. The young man is seen from behind and the side, his face mostly hidden. Worn corners.
